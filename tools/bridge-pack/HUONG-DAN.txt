========================================================================
 BRIDGE MAY CHAM CONG -> HRM
 Cai tren may tinh nam CUNG MANG LAN voi may cham cong.
========================================================================

MAY NAO CAI
-----------
Phai la may:
  - Bat 24/7 (hoac it nhat suot gio lam)
  - Cung dai mang voi may cham cong: 192.168.110.x
  - Khong doi mang (dung laptop mang di mang lai se dut lien tuc)

May DESKTOP-4E1ET1Q (192.168.110.58) dang chay Ronald Jack Pro la lua
chon dung: no da nam san tren mang do va bat ca ngay.


CAN GI
------
Chi can Node.js tu phien ban 20 tro len. Tai ban LTS tai nodejs.org.
KHONG can npm install, khong can chep node_modules - moi thu da nam
trong file attendance-bridge.mjs roi.


CAI DAT - 3 BUOC
----------------
1. Chep ca thu muc nay vao may do. Vi du: C:\hrm-bridge

2. Mo PowerShell bang CHUOT PHAI > "Run as administrator".
   Khong lam buoc nay thi script se bao thieu quyen va dung lai.

3. Go hai dong:

      cd C:\hrm-bridge
      powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File cai-dat.ps1

Xong. Bridge chay ngay, va tu chay lai moi lan may khoi dong.


KIEM TRA CHAY DUNG CHUA
-----------------------
      node --env-file=.env.attendance-bridge kiem-tra.mjs

No di lan luot sau muc va dung ngay cho hong, kem cach sua:
  1. Bien moi truong    4. Danh sach ma nhan vien tren may
  2. Duong mang         5. Vai lan cham gan nhat
  3. Bat tay thiet bi   6. Token tren Supabase

Xem log dang chay:
      Get-Content logs\bridge.log -Tail 30 -Wait


KHI MAY CHAM CONG DOI DIA CHI IP
--------------------------------
May dang lay IP qua DHCP nen dia chi se troi. Troi la bridge mat dau may
va ngung chay du lieu, KHONG co canh bao nao.

Tim lai dia chi that:
      node --env-file=.env.attendance-bridge tim-may.mjs

Thay dia chi moi thi sua dong RJ_DEVICE_IP trong .env.attendance-bridge,
roi khoi dong lai:
      Stop-ScheduledTask  -TaskName "HRM Attendance Bridge"
      Start-ScheduledTask -TaskName "HRM Attendance Bridge"

Cach dut diem: dat IP TINH cho may cham cong (Menu > Comm > Ethernet),
hoac dat reservation tren router theo MAC 00:17:61:11:4D:F6.


THEO DOI TU XA
--------------
Tren HRM > May cham cong co dong "Bridge bao danh", cap nhat moi 20 giay
khi bridge con song:

      Dang chay        -> binh thuong
      N phut truoc     -> bridge vua dung
      ngay gio cu the  -> da dung lau

Day la cach phan biet "bridge chet" voi "may cham cong tat" - hai chuyen
khac nhau nhung truoc day nhin giong het nhau tren man hinh.


LUU Y
-----
- File .env.attendance-bridge chua TOKEN va comm key. Dung gui qua chat
  hay email cong khai.
- CHI cai bridge tren MOT may. May cham cong chi phuc vu mot ket noi;
  hai bridge cung goi thi mot cai bao mat ket noi.
- Du lieu khong mat khi bridge dung: may tu luu trong bo nho (hien
  7372/150000 ban ghi). Bridge chay lai se keo ve 30 ngay gan nhat.
